The Preface

Dear our Customers

Foreword, Na Nu SAI GON PRINTING COMPANY LIMITED would like to send our best wishes to health, success and properity. We thank customers for your trust, and cooperation with us in many years.

NNSP was established and developed in 2008, beside the main factory located in HCMC, the company continued to develop a factory at Binh Dinh to confirm the mark and goal to become the Top 1 in Vietnam in the field of printing.

With solid business strategies, a strong investment in technology, and the passion for individual work, Na Nu has taken a new high-profile position in the industry. Printing on fashion, sports, and many other items

NNSP specializes in printing on high-class products, famous brands all over the world.

Na Nu’s goal is to print products that will always made our customers satisfy on term good quality and fastest delivery.

We are committed to print products with a wide variety of products at competitive prices, with the best quality compared to other competitors in the field.

With the motto: “CUSTOMER SATISFACTION IS THE KEY TO OUR SUCCESS”, each individual in the company, we always consciousness the importance of each job that they are assumed.

Best regards!
